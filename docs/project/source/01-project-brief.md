# Quick Expense Notes: project brief

## Purpose
Let a person note an expense in a few characters, such as `SWG 340 yd`, and get a clean expense record and a monthly summary by category.

## Primary user
Asha, a working professional who tracks her daily spending on a laptop.

## What the user does
1. Signs in.
2. Types one or more short entries into a single text box, one per line or separated by semicolons.
3. Reviews the expanded entries, then saves them.
4. Edits or deletes an entry that is wrong.
5. Opens the monthly summary to see the total for each category and the grand total.

## Goals
- Expand every short entry exactly as the abbreviation rules say.
- Make entering many expenses fast.
- Show monthly totals that always match the saved entries.

## Non-goals
- No bank or UPI sync.
- No receipt upload.
- No budgets or alerts.
- No sharing between users.
- No guessing: an unknown code is an error, not a suggestion.

## Success
- Every entry in `03-sample-entries.md` expands exactly as shown there.
- The monthly summary totals match the saved entries.

## Notes
- One user per account. There are no organisations or tenants.
- The rules are in `02-abbreviation-rules.md`.
